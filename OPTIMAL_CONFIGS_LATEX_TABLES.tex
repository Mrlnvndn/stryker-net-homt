\section*{Methodology Notes}

\paragraph{Data Source:} Analysis based on GLM binomial ANOVA with logit link applied to SSHOM success rates from 5 repositories: CsvHelper, GuardClauses, Marsen.NetCore.Dojo.Integration.Test, MoreLinq, and TimeProviderExtensions. Total of 10 algorithm×repository combinations (2 algorithms × 5 repositories).

\paragraph{Separation Issue:} GuardClauses-Genetic exhibits quasi-complete separation with extremely large improvement factor (8.86e+16) and infinite confidence intervals. This indicates the model achieves near-perfect prediction for this combination. While the predicted optimal configuration is reported, statistical estimates should be interpreted cautiously.

\paragraph{Improvement Factor:} Calculated as multiplicative gain in predicted odds of SSHOM success with optimal heuristics vs no heuristics: $\text{Improvement} = \frac{\text{OR}_{\text{optimal config}}}{\text{OR}_{\text{all OFF}}}$ where OR is the odds ratio from the GLM model.

\paragraph{Statistical Significance:} All configurations selected based on exhaustive search of $2^6=64$ possible combinations per algorithm×repository. Predictions derived from GLM coefficients with FDR-corrected $q$-values for significance testing. Only statistically significant effects included in optimal configurations.

\paragraph{Universal Configuration:} Two thresholds applied: (1) Core universal config includes heuristics appearing in ≥70\% of optimal configs (CodeLocation, EmptyAssessingTests, MaxSizeLimit); (2) Extended universal config includes heuristics at ≥50\% threshold (adds OverlappingTests and MutatorType).

\paragraph{Reproducibility:} All results generated via \texttt{analyze\_heuristic\_performance.py} and \texttt{analyze\_universal\_config.py} scripts. CSV exports available in \texttt{part4\_best\_configs\_*.csv} files (5 files, one per repository, each containing rankings for both algorithms).
